# CADD

**Combined Annotation Dependent Depletion** scores the deleteriousness of single-nucleotide variants and small indels by integrating many genomic annotations into one score.

## Overview
- **Task:** variant effect prediction (deleterious vs. neutral)
- **Input:** variant (chrom, pos, ref, alt)
- **Output:** raw score and PHRED-scaled score
